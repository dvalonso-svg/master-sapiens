Master Sapiens
ENES

Adult Animated Miniseries · 5 × 18-minute episodes (approx.)

An original concept and script by David Alonso

History is not a monument to contemplate. It is a biological archive to be mined to engineer a “perfect future”.

London, 1890. In an isolated Gothic abbey, the Knowledge Club safeguards human knowledge against the collapse of time. Then reality begins to change.

Behind the anomaly are the Master Sapiens: assimilated beings from the future, led by Buffon D., who cross time to extract the talent of history’s great minds.

Executive production and animation: 12 Pingüinos

The Knowledge Club

Shrouded in London’s winter fog, a desolate abbey of iron and glass rises like a vast Victorian glasshouse—part library, part botanical garden.

It edits

The threat doesn’t invade; it edits. Wood flattens, stone becomes code, architecture folds in on itself.

Why 2D

Adult 2D animation. Minimalist ligne claire rooted in European auteur comics.
Across six stages of degradation, Victorian space becomes a two-dimensional data diagram.

Algorithm Blue

Bronze, wood, soot and cold greys meet a flat, hypnotic blue: the signal of extraction and assimilation.

Science-fiction thriller · Biopunk · Psychological horror

H. G. Wells

The series’ moral compass—not an innocent witness.

Guardian of the time machine and of the belief that knowledge must be preserved, not extracted.

Buffon D.

They cross time to extract knowledge—not to preserve it.

A former founder of the Knowledge Club, he leads the Master Sapiens across time.

Master Sapiens

Assimilated beings from the future. Extraction and assimilation turn the threat inward: Clerics become Master Sapiens.

A universe beyond the series

Master Sapiens is conceived as a narrative universe. Two companion works share its chronology and visual language.

Hybrid novelThe 2027 Manuscript

Madrid, 2027. A geneticist receives a nineteenth-century document signed by H. G. Wells. Her investigation uncovers a centuries-old technology for extracting historical talent. Fiction, essay and “Drafts” in a single volume.

Graphic albumThe Map of Chrono-Colonization

In parallel, the Master Sapiens move against five nodes of human knowledge: Hypatia, Plato, Ibn al-Haytham, Sor Juana Inés and Marie Curie. A chronology of facts described via 60 pages approx. of bidimensional image sequences.
